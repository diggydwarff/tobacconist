## Tobacconist

**Tobacconist** is a comprehensive tobacco farming, processing, automation, and smoking mod for Minecraft. Grow multiple tobacco varieties, manage crop quality, cure leaves using Air, Sun, Fire, or Flue methods, ferment and age tobacco, create different cuts and blends, and produce cigarettes, cigars, pipe tobacco, aromatic tobacco, molasses, Shisha, and more. The mod includes pipes and Hookahs, specialized processing equipment, storage, villagers, advancements, an in-game manual, and extensive factory automation while keeping traditional manual production fully viable.

## Compatibility

* **Create 6** — Extensive automation, logistics, processing, Ponder, and factory integration
* **JEI** — Recipe and processing integration
* **Patchouli** — Full in-game Tobacconist's Manual
* **Curios API** — Mouth-slot smoking and Spectacles support
* **Farmer's Delight**
* **Fruits Delight**
* **Croptopia**
* **Neapolitan**
* **HerbalBrews**
* **Farmer's Respite**
* **Expanded Delight**
* **Rustic Delight**
* **Coffee Delight**
* **Create Confectionery**

Additional food and farming mods may work automatically through shared **`c:`** and compatible **`forge:`** ingredient tags.
