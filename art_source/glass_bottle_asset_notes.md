# Sized glass bottle assets

The Small and Large Glass Bottle sprites derive from the Minecraft 1.21.1
`minecraft:textures/item/glass_bottle.png` pixels (Mojang/Microsoft).
They retain the source colors and transparency, resampled with nearest-neighbor
scaling into a 16x16 item canvas. The visible small silhouette is 7x10 pixels;
the large silhouette is 13x15 pixels. Filled variants layer a neutral interior
mask beneath the glass and tint that mask using the stored fluid.

The Bubble Pipe and Liquid Blending Bench item sprites are the user-supplied
16x16 PNG files copied unchanged. The placed bench retains its earlier supplied
Blockbench model and atlas.
