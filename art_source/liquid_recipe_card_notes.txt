Liquid Recipe Card, alpha 5

This is a pixel-preserving recolor of Create's 16x16 schematic item, using
Minecraft paper's warm highlights and three short list marks. It is no longer
the generated icon from alpha 4. The original schematic outline, alpha mask,
folds and native 16x16 grid are retained.

Sources: vanilla assets/minecraft/textures/item/paper.png and
Create assets/create/textures/item/schematic.png, from the development JARs.
Native output: assets/tobacconistmod/textures/item/liquid_recipe_card.png.
Palette: muted sage green with warm paper highlights and dark green folds.
