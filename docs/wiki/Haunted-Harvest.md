# Haunted Harvest Update

Haunted Harvest expands tobacco processing with a seasonal Haunted cure, traditional specialty tobacco styles, new autumn flavor profiles, hidden blends, and a corrected Plug/Flake production chain.

## Haunted tobacco

Raw tobacco can be Haunted-cured over a lit Soul Campfire. With Create installed, suitable soul-fire Haunting airflow provides the automated equivalent. Haunted tobacco has no inherent power/quality advantage; it exists for presentation and recipe/blend discovery. Smoke from Haunted tobacco may include restrained soul-fire wisps, and finished cigars/cigarettes carrying Haunted tobacco use Haunted naming.

The exact seasonal secret-blend recipes are intentionally not documented. The six included blends are **Witching Hour**, **Graveyard Watch**, **Headless Horseman**, **Harvest Moon**, **Maple Hollow**, and **All Hallows' Eve**. Witching Hour and Graveyard Watch are non-aromatic; the others mix vanilla-compatible and optional mod-supplied autumn flavors.

## Autumn flavors

Haunted Harvest adds flavor profiles for Maple, Nutmeg, Ginger, Clove, and Pecan. Flavor ingredient lookup is tag-first so compatible ingredients from other mods can participate without becoming hard dependencies.

## Traditional styles

The update also adds Latakia, Dark Fired Kentucky, Perique, Cavendish, Black Cavendish, and Stoved Virginia. See [Specialty Tobacco Processing](Specialty-Tobacco-Processing.md) for the vanilla and Create processing routes.

## Plug and Flake

Flake now has a proper two-stage preparation: Rough Cut is compressed into Pressed Plug, then the Plug is sliced with a Chaveta. Vanilla uses a downward-facing powered piston pressing into a Tobacco Barrel; Create can use a Mechanical Press.

## Seasonal smoking equipment

The **Jack-o'-Lantern Hookah** is a two-block seasonal Hookah crafted from a standard Hookah and a Jack o'Lantern. It uses the same fuel, water, Shisha, hose, inventory, Hopper/Create logistics, and Display Link behavior as the other tall Hookahs.

The **Corn Cob Pipe** is a rustic reusable pipe for the harvest season and follows the normal pipe packing and smoking rules.
